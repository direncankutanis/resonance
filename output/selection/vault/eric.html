1 · Separate your money

All amounts are Demo RLO. Current cash excludes flexible savings and money already in goals. Income means money available for this period, not a promise of future income.

2 · Fund obligations in your chosen order

Priority 1 goes first. Ties use due day, then row order. A bill is funded in full or left unfunded; later smaller bills may still fit. Due days only order this single-period proposal, not schedule payments.

Checking an expense alone does not enable savings use. Your protected reserve and goal balances are never fallback sources.

3 · Give the remainder a purpose

Goals share remaining cash in priority order. Completed goals stop receiving allocations. Any unmet obligation or reserve deficit pauses all new goal contributions.
