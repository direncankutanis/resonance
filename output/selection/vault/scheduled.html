Try it / About one minute

Two purchases, two steps apart.

The default plan reserves 20 credits for two 10-credit purchases. Review and confirm, then advance: step 1 waits, step 2 buys, step 3 waits, step 4 buys again.

What if I pause, or the price rises?

Pause before a due step or set the price above your limit. That opportunity is skipped and its 10 credits returned. The next opportunity stays on the original schedule. There are no catch-up purchases. Stale price data also skips a due opportunity.

This explicit skip policy belongs to this prototype. It is not a claim that every recurring transaction or Rialo application uses the same policy.
