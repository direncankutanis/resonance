    What makes this useful outside the rehearsal?

    One budget can govern several automated purchases. A new week renews the period allowance, but it does not refill your balance or spend the reserve. A stale quote pauses an order; a later fresh quote can qualify again.

    In a real implementation these checks must run together at execution, using trusted time and data. A frontend warning alone cannot protect funds. The rehearsal uses deterministic local rules, not live Rialo execution. Ece’s AI assistant only drafts your stated boundaries.
