How this connects to the lesson and Rialo

In the lesson: shield below threshold → transfer reserve. Here: price at or below limit → attempt a budgeted purchase. The condition changes; the reasoning stays the same.

Rialo describes predicates evaluated within native block execution, with triggered transactions queued for execution. This demo models that sequence manually. Its steps are not Rialo blocks, and it does not validate network availability or integration.

A real version also requires verified NFT ownership, explicit spending authorization, a reliable price source, liquidity, transaction fees and execution-time checks. Triggering a rule is not a guarantee that a purchase succeeds.
