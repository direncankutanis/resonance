What is live?

A clear boundary between learning and execution.

Wallet connection and NFT ownership use Sepolia. The lesson and vault purchases are simulations: no real trading or Rialo mainnet execution.

The test NFT contract does not verify gameplay onchain. Completing the quiz unlocks this app’s mint flow.

AI & Latch

Live AI drafts and Latch proposal checks currently run in our local development preview. This public release offers guided examples and manual rules; hosted service access is a later step.
